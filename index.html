Inside your routine

What each step does for your skin

A simple routine has four steps. Here is what happens at the surface of your skin at each one.

  1. Step 01 of 04

    Cleanse

    Bubbles lift oil, dirt, sunscreen and make-up off the surface of the skin, so they rinse away.

    In the range: 19 products

    CleansersWaters and toners

  2. Step 02 of 04

    Treat

    A serum is light and concentrated. It soaks into the outer layer of the skin, before your moisturiser.

    In the range: 13 products

    SerumsExfoliators and treatments

  3. Step 03 of 04

    Moisturise

    Moisturiser forms a soft layer on top that helps hold water in, so skin feels comfortable.

    In the range: 29 products

    MoisturisersBody careRepair and balms

  4. Step 04 of 04

    Protect

    Sunscreen forms a layer that filters most of the sun’s UV rays before they reach your skin. It goes on last, every morning.

    In the range: 13 products

    Sunscreens

of UVB rays filtered by SPF 30

SPF in numbers

What SPF 30 actually blocks

Sunscreen with SPF 30 filters about 97% of the sun's UVB rays. SPF 50 filters about 98%.

CANSA recommends sunscreen with SPF 20 to 50, and 30 to 50 for fair skin.

Sources: The Skin Cancer Foundation and CANSA, Be SunSmart.
