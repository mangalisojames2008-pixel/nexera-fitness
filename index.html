02 · The number

Thirty minutes. Five days. That's the start.

The World Health Organization recommends that adults get at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, like a brisk walk or an easy cycle. That is about 30 minutes, five days a week.

Worldwide, 31% of adults do not reach it. A short walk today counts.

Source: World Health Organization, physical activity fact sheet
